# Q2: Where does the instability start, and how much cold frit cures it?

Commit c5027a2 (uncommitted changes present), procedure version 2 (see questions.py). Ji et al. element, uniform power, flow scaled with power, 2.5% power steps from 5% to 100% (1 to 20 MW/L).

## Answer (channel and plenum on)
- **No cold frit:** no steady solution at 1.5 to 20.0 MW/L (38 points); unstable at 1.0 to 1.0 MW/L (1 points). The bottom slice is starved by flow shift and overheats past the 5000 K property limit.
- **Cold frit share that cures it:** between 0.754 and 0.757 (every power from 1 to 20 MW/L converged and stable above this share). The published frit's share is 0.903.
- Frits below the cure share fail at high power first: share 0.50: no solution at 5.5 to 20.0 MW/L (30 points); share 0.70: no solution at 15.0 to 20.0 MW/L (11 points).

## Context (channel effects off, Kerrebrock's parallel-passage setup)
- **No cold frit:** unstable from 1.0 to 16.5 MW/L, stable from 17.0 MW/L up.
- **Cure:** between share 0.20 (still unstable) and 0.30 (cured).

## Hypotheses
- **H2** (no cold frit -> static instability below about 4 MW/L): reproduced in form, not in number. The model's instability is below a threshold, but at 16.5 to 17.0 MW/L, about four times Kerrebrock's value.
- **H3** (a sufficient cold frit removes it): confirmed in both setups.
