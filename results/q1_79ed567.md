# Q1: Does power density decide whether cold- or hot-frit control works?

Commit 79ed567. Five frit designs per element with the total frit pressure drop held at the published value, 1 to 30 MW/L in 0.5 MW/L steps, uniform power. Only stability is tested (no cross-flow).

## Answer
- **Ji et al. element: Partly.** Cold-dominated (published, fraction 0.93): stable at every power density. Hot-dominated (mirrored, fraction 0.07): unstable 1.0 to 10.5 MW/L (20 points); no solution 11.0 to 30.0 MW/L (39 points); stable at 0 of 59 power densities.
- **Tsinghua element: Partly.** Cold-dominated (published, fraction 0.96): stable at every power density. Hot-dominated (mirrored, fraction 0.04): unstable 1.0 to 30.0 MW/L (59 points); stable at 0 of 59 power densities.
- In this model, cold-frit control works at every power density tested and hot-frit control does not work at low power density, so power density does not decide which works. This contradicts the low-power half of Ji et al.'s explanation, for stability.

## Hypothesis
- **H4** (at 20-30 MW/L, hot-frit-dominated unstable, cold-frit-dominated stable): Ji et al. element: confirmed; Tsinghua element: confirmed.

## Limits of this answer
- Only stability is tested. Ji et al. may prefer hot-frit control at low power density for other reasons (temperature uniformity, pressure drop) that this question does not test.
- The design family holds the total frit pressure drop at the published value. Hot-frit designs with a different total were not explored.
