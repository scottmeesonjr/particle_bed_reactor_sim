# Q4: Does Ji et al.'s point A stay stable from 20% to 100% throttle?

Commit 7659096. Cosine power, channel and plenum on, flow scaled with power, 2.5% steps. No hypothesis was made.

## Answer
- **Yes.** Point A is stable at every swept power from 20% to 100% and also down to 5%. No point failed to converge.
- **Smaller stability margin.** Its least-stable eigenvalue is 0.50 to 0.67 times the published frit's across the operational range, so disturbances die out more slowly.
- **Thermal advantage reverses at low throttle.** Point A runs cooler than the published frit at high power, but hotter below 5.5 MW/L, and above the 3,800 K fuel limit at 20.0%, 22.5% power (within the operational range).
